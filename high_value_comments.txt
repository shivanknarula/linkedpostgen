High SKU complexity

Scale + speed without compromising safety

After teaching hundreds of students in robotics workshops, I've noticed the same three ROS mistakes repeated again and again.
And they're not syntax errors.
They're thinking errors.
1. Learning commands without understanding architecture Many learners can create a node, publish a topic, and launch a package.
But ask them how data flows through a robot, and they struggle.
ROS is not about memorizing commands.
It's about understanding how components communicate and work together.
2. Jumping straight into projects
Everyone wants to build an autonomous robot on Day 1.
Very few spend time understanding:
• Nodes
• Topics
• Services
• Actions
• TF
Without these fundamentals, debugging becomes frustrating.
3. Ignoring visualization and debugging tools
Tools like RViz, rqt_graph, and ros2 topic echo are not optional.
Professional robotics engineers spend a significant amount of time observing and debugging system behavior before writing more code.
The students who progress fastest aren't always the best programmers.
They're the ones who understand the system as a whole.
ROS becomes much easier when you stop thinking about code and start thinking about communication, data flow, and architecture.
