From a single camera to full sensor fusion — here's how I upgraded my robot simulation.
A few weeks ago I built my first ROS2 robot simulation with just a camera sensor. It worked — but a real robot doesn't perceive the world through one sensor alone.
So I went back and upgraded it.
🏭 Multi-Sensor Warehouse Robot Simulation
Same robot. Completely new capabilities.
I integrated 3 sensors running simultaneously:
📷 Camera — Live RGB image stream
📡 LiDAR — 360° laser scanning, 12m range, visualized as a live point cloud
📐 IMU — Orientation & acceleration tracking at 50Hz with realistic noise simulation
The world was upgraded to a full warehouse environment — walls, industrial shelves, stacked boxes and ceiling lighting — making the sensor data actually meaningful.
🔧 What I learned:
→ Managing TF2 transform trees across multiple sensor frames
→ Bridging Gazebo topics to ROS2 using ros_gz_bridge
→ Tuning sensor noise for realistic simulation
🛠️ Stack: ROS2 Jazzy • Gazebo Harmonic • RViz2 • URDF/Xacro • ros_gz_bridge • tf2_ros

🔹 Disrupt — Understand how the future belongs to autonomous industrial ecosystems driven by Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning.
