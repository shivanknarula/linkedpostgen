Autonomous AI agents that can provision infrastructure and escalate privileges, but no way to stop them when they go off-script.
That's the state of most agent deployments today.

Three things every AI kill switch needs:
1. Cryptographic identity revocation (not just "delete the session")
2. Cascade blocking — if Agent A spawned Agent B, revoking A should revoke B
3. Audit trail — you need to know what it did between "go" and "stop"
Most companies are deploying agents with none of these. They'll learn why they matter the hard way.
What's your agent kill switch strategy? If you don't have one, that's the answer.
